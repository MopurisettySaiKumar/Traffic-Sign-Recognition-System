📘 About the project

A Traffic Sign Recognition (TSR) system is a computer vision technology used in advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles to detect and classify roadside traffic signs from images and video.

01 Acquisition

Upload an image, video or capture camera frames.

02 Preprocessing

Resize and normalize frames for the model.

03 Detection

YOLO predicts traffic sign bounding boxes.

04 Classification

Display model class labels and confidence scores.

Important: Supported classes depend on the pretrained model and training dataset. Not all Indian or international signs are supported. Not for real driving decisions.
